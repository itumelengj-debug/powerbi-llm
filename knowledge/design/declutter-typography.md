# Decluttering and typography

## Remove what does not carry data
- Axis titles when the visual title already names the measure and grain.
- Gridlines, or keep only light dotted value gridlines.
- Value axes on bar charts that show data labels.
- Borders and shadows on every visual: a light 1 px border with rounded corners or a subtle background
  separates visuals better than heavy outlines.
- Legends for single-series charts; for few series, label the lines directly or put the legend top-left.
- Decimals beyond what decisions need.

## Titles that say something
A title names the measure and the grain ("Notional by month, current year") or states the insight
("Notional fell 12% after March"). Keep titles short, sentence case, left-aligned and consistent in size.

## Type scale
One font family (Segoe UI is the Power BI default and renders everywhere). A practical scale: page title
20 pt bold, subtitle 11 pt, visual titles 12 pt semibold, KPI values 26 to 28 pt semibold, table text and
headers 10 pt, axis and data labels 9 pt. Never go below 9 pt. Set these in the theme's textClasses and
visualStyles so every visual follows them.

## Spacing
Use the 8 px grid and 16 px gutters, 8 px internal padding, and align text baselines in a row. White
space between groups is what makes a page look calm, not empty.
